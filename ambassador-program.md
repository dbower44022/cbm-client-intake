# The Ambassador Program — user guide

Last Updated: 11-21-26 22:59 · Revision 1.0 — see change log at the end.

How members log what they do to spread the word, and how the program team
answers them. What the program is for and what the application does with
each report is in *The Ambassador Program — how it works*
(`ambassador-program-overview.md`). A hands-on walkthrough is the training
course *The Ambassador Program — demo* in the application.

## Part 1 — For members

### Opening the page

Open **Ambassador Activity**: the tile among your applications, or **Log an
activity** under the greeting on the home page. On a phone, bookmark the
page.

If you are not signed in, the page shows a sign-in box in place: your CRM
username and password, **Show** to check what you typed, and **Forgot your
password?**, which emails you a link to choose a new one. After signing in,
the form appears on the same page.

### Logging an activity

1. **When did you do it?** Today is filled in; change it if needed. A date
   in the future is refused.
2. **What did you do?** Choose one of the six buttons.
3. **Who did you talk to?** Shown for every activity except liking or
   sharing a post, and required for a referral.
   - Type the person's **email address** and move to the next box. If we
     already know the person, their name, organization, title and phone
     fill in, tinted, with *From our records.* If the address's domain
     belongs to a company we know, the organization fills in; if several
     companies share the domain, pick one. Personal addresses such as Gmail
     never fill in a company.
   - Or type three or more letters of their **first or last name** and
     pick them from the list.
   - **Everything stays editable.** If something we filled in is wrong,
     change it: your change goes to the program team, and our records are
     not changed by it. **Not this person** clears what was filled in.
   - Typing a fellow member's address shows *That's a … member*: members
     are not recorded as contacts.
4. **This person is…** a potential client, a potential partner, a potential
   funder, from an organization we already work with, or someone else. When
   the person's organization is one of our partners, this is answered for
   you.
5. **Did they agree to hear from …?** (the organization's short name) Shown for a potential client, partner
   or funder. Answer **Yes** only if they agreed to receive our mail.
6. **Does … need to follow up?** (the organization's short name) Yes or No. If Yes, say in
   the next box what help you need.
7. **Anything we should know?** An event or organization name helps. Keep
   client details and confidential mentoring information out.
8. **Save my report.** A button is never greyed out: if something is
   missing, a message under the form names it.

The thank-you shows a reference such as `AMB-7K2M9QXA`. It is the same for
every report: it never says where the person was found.

### My activity

Under the form, **My activity** lists your own reports, newest first: the
date, the activity, the person, the follow-up (*Requested* or *Done*) and,
for a referral, whether they have applied (*Not yet* or *Applied*).

- **Correct** changes the date, the activity or the note of a report for 14
  days after you saved it. After that, **Correct** says so: ask the program
  team.
- A report still being recorded shows *Saving…* for a moment.
- A person or company created from your report cannot be removed here: ask
  the program team.

### The emails you may receive

- *Your ambassador follow-up was handled*, with the program team's note.
- *Your referral applied*, when someone you referred applies.

## Part 2 — For the program team

### The review list

Open **Ambassador Review** (a tile among your applications; the home page's
**Ambassador follow-up** row opens the oldest request). Each row says what
it needs:

- **Follow-up** — a member asked for help. Act on it, write in **What was
  done** what you did, and press **Mark follow-up done**. The member is
  emailed your note.
- **Contact to place** — the application could not place the person. Either:
  - choose **A partner**, **A funder** or **A client's engagement**, type
    part of the record's name, and pick it: an Ambassador Contact session is
    recorded there and its manager or mentor is told; or
  - **Create as a new client, partner or funder referral**: the person is
    created and credited to the member (an email address is needed); or
  - type why in **Or dismiss it: why?** and press **Dismiss**.
- **Correction** — a member changed something we hold. If they are right,
  change the person or company in its own screen, then press **Mark as
  read**.

Every action is recorded in the action history, on the member's mentor
profile. If the CRM refuses to store something, the list says which fields;
nothing is ever reported as done when it was not.

### The figures

**Analytics → Ambassador Program**: active ambassadors, activities reported
and by type, open follow-ups, contacts awaiting review, referrals made and
applied, and contacts recorded on known records. Most figures are kept for
an hour; the page's own **Refresh** button works them out again.

### A member's history

**Mentor Administration → the member → Ambassador** lists every report the
member made, read-only.

## Part 3 — For administrators

- **Switching it on:** System Settings → *Ambassador Program*. Switching it
  off hides every screen; the records stay.
- **Who logs, who answers:** System Settings → Team gates → *Ambassador
  Activity — members* (default Mentor Team) and *Ambassador Activity —
  program team* (default Marketing Admin Team).
- **Other settings:** the email domains never matched to a company, the
  14-day correction window, and how often the worker retries a report that
  could not be recorded at once.
- **The CRM build** each chapter needs before switching on:
  `ambassador-activity-crm-handoff.md`.
