Recipe

Steps run in order. Add matching widens the list; Keep only matching narrows the members of its own type; Remove matching takes them out. Conditions in one step must all match — for "either … or", use two Add steps. A step can also add or remove the members of another list (a standing exclusion list), or add the records a file names.
